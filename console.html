Poppy said something wrong

  1. Find the reply in Activity and hit Flag as wrong.
  2. Reply to the customer yourself in Gorgias with the correct answer.
  3. Flagged replies get reviewed and the agent's knowledge is corrected.

Something feels off — stop it

  1. Set the switch at the top to Paused. That's it.
  2. Emails simply wait for the team in Gorgias, like before.
  3. No permission needed. When in doubt, pause.

A customer is upset they got an AI reply

  1. Take over the ticket in Gorgias — the agent backs off any ticket a person is assigned to.
  2. Answer personally, warmly, as always.
  3. Flag the original reply so we can review it.

Teach it something new

  1. Open the Knowledge tab.
  2. Edit an article, or add one, written the way you'd tell a customer.
  3. Save. The very next ticket uses it.
